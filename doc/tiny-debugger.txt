1. Introduction                                 *tiny-debugger-introduction*

tiny-debugger.nvim gives you a fully-featured debugging experience out of one
line of config. Under the hood it wires together nvim-dap, nvim-dap-ui,
nvim-dap-virtual-text, and persistent-breakpoints into a single cohesive
experience with modal keybindings.
